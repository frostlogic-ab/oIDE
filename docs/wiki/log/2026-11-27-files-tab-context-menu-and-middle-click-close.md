## [2026-11-27] build | Files tab context menu and middle-click close

Open-file tabs in Files get a right-click menu (also Menu key / Shift+F10): Close, Close all to the
right, Close all. Middle click closes a tab. Bulk closes ask once about unsaved changes and go
through the new `useFiles.closeTabs`. No telemetry event: a small editor convenience that answers no
product question. Page: [screen-files](../pages/screen-files.md).
